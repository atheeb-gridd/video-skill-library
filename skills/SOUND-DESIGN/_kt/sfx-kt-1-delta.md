# Delta — SFX KT 1 (catalogue of named sound-effect families)

## What materially improved

The baseline captured 9 families and lost the 10th entirely. The two new passes recover **Foley sound effects as the named 10th family** — with its definition ("fake sound effects that sound completely real, but they're actually recorded sitting inside a studio"), its example set (footsteps, clothes, door creak) and the fact that the whole DIY-recording demo from 00:07:27 to 00:09:36 is *inside* the Foley section, not a loose appendix. That reframes the second half of the video. The baseline also mis-numbered whip as the 10th item (it is 9th), lost the material variations of hit & impact (metal, wood), lost the whip+whoosh layering trick, lost the entire free-sources block (freesound.org, Pixabay, and the copyright-claim motivation for it), lost the single most actionable library rule in the video ("don't use the same whoosh three or four times in a row"), and garbled two cartoon effect names (`point` → **boing**, `[unclear]` → **whistle**). Inside the DIY demo the new passes name the actual Premiere effect (**Pitch Shifter**) and reveal that the pitch is first raised and *then* lowered to 0.6, which the baseline collapsed into a single downward move. The new passes also recover the second Foley recipe in full: **bone break = wood breaking + water splash**.

Where the passes disagree: the v2 Hinglish pass is preferred throughout. Both new passes truncate the whoosh-vs-swoosh distinction at 00:01:32 ("both sound effects are really the movement of air…"), and only the older pass carries the payoff line at 00:01:53 and the entire intimate-sounds definition at 00:05:34-00:05:49 — those cues are marked `[older pass only]` in the transcript rather than dropped.
